🐝 Mystery at Honeybees Library 🔎

Solo Detective • Digital Referee • TBR Takedown

📁 Start a case

Select which cards can be the secret solution. All nine rooms stay available for physical board movement and suggestions. Excluded cards are known from the beginning.

A case is saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data can erase it.

🕵️ Active investigation

Use your physical board and dice to move. When you enter a room, tap Record visit, then make a suggestion using that room. The referee checks only cards matching your suggestion. You can investigate excluded rooms, too.

🏠 Room tracker

🔎 Make a suggestion

Select the room you physically entered, plus any participating suspect and object. Repeating an identical suggestion always gives the same result.

📝 Detective notebook

Known exclusions are crossed out. Confirmed evidence is green. Tap any other card to mark a personal note (a ?), then tap again to clear it.

📜 Investigation history

    ⚖️ Final accusation

    An incorrect accusation won't reveal the solution; you may continue investigating. A correct accusation closes the case and reveals your TBR genre.
